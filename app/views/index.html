About ECMS

ECMS is part of the wider group of components that forms Customer Communications and Media Services (CCAMS). The other components are Notifications and Preferences.

ECMS (Enterprise Content Management Service) is the department’s strategic solution for storing and managing content related to a citizen’s interaction with DWP. It supports the entry, validation, storage, retrieval, and deletion of evidence such as forms, medical documents, and letters, while complying with DWP policy and GDPR. Documents are received through the Mail Opening Unit (MOU), manual uploads via the DRS Portal, and digital applications.
